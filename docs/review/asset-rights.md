## License evidence

The copyright page of source/original.pdf (PDF page 4, unnumbered) identifies the book as an open-access publication under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). It expressly includes images and other third-party material unless an individual credit line indicates otherwise. All nine chapter notices repeat that inclusion rule: PDF pages 30, 60, 112, 141, 168, 190, 222, 272 and 285.

No individual line excluding a reused interior figure or table from that license was found. A citation, data-source credit, or statement that an item was reproduced/adopted from another source identifies its source; none of the lines below states a different license or an exclusion. Accordingly, the interior assets are treated as included under the supplied book/chapter notices. The earlier proposed provenance investigations for Table 4.1 and Figures 9.3 and 9.7 are closed on this evidence.

Translation and figure-label localization are adaptations of the licensed material. CC BY 4.0 includes translations and other modifications in its definition of adapted material, permits producing and sharing adaptations, and requires retaining supplied attribution/notices, marking changes and providing the license link. See Sections 1(a), 2(a)(1) and 3(a) of the official [CC BY 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en). Attribution for this adaptation retains chapter authors, copyright notices, source DOI/license links, bibliography and the credits below; translation, localization and typesetting changes are to be identified in the edition notice.

This determination records the notices supplied with this particular book. It does not relabel independently obtained copies of the cited source works as CC BY 4.0.
